# Part 3 — Business Case: Bill Banayein

**Pankaj Khannade · Fixxly Head of Product Assignment**  
**May 2026**

---

## Recommendation

**Prioritize Bill Banayein** for the next 90 days.

Fixxly already wins **order 1**. It loses **order 2 and beyond** because the **hardware dealer** still owns the client material list. Bill Banayein places Fixxly at **job start** (hisaab → order), which increases **frequency and retention** among existing contractors — the fastest lever to move from **60 to 150 OPD per store** without a catalogue war or paid-acquisition blitz.

*All figures use the assumptions stated below. Update MAC, CAC, and contribution with Fixxly actuals.*

## Path to 150 OPD

One store, 90 days. Three product-led moves — not increased paid traffic.

| Step | What changes | OPD / day |
|------|--------------|-----------|
| **Today** | 200 contractors × 9 orders/month | **60** |
| **+ Bill habit** | Same contractors order more often (9 → 12/month) | **80** |
| **+ Retention** | More contractors stay active (200 → 280 MAC) | **112** |
| **+ Deeper habit** | 280 → 300 MAC; 12 → 15 orders/month | **150** |

Bill Banayein directly drives **frequency** (steps 1→2) and **retention** (2→3). Step 4 is the 90-day operating target.

## Why Bill Banayein before anything else

**1.** Fixxly’s 40% non-repeat problem is **structural** — order 2 and beyond goes to the **hardware dealer** because the app never owned **client material hisaab** — and Bill Banayein intercepts that moment **before** the dealer receives the WhatsApp list.

**2.** It raises **orders per contractor** (9 → 15) and **retention** among the **~200 contractors already in each store’s catchment** — without waiting for 3× SKU count or a catalogue war with HomeRun.

**3.** At **~₹1,100 CAC** and **₹180/order contribution**, that frequency lift improves payback from **~0.7 to ~0.4 months** and GMV per store from **₹36L to ₹90L** — the fastest credible path to **150 OPD in 90 days** because it attacks **habit**, not acquisition spend.

---

## Secondary priorities

- **Coupons / cashback** — buys a repeat click; does not own the job workflow  
- **“Reorder last basket”** — wrong for contractors on a **new site every day**  
- **Catalogue expansion vs HomeRun** — Fixxly’s wedge is **30 min + workflow**, not SKU count  

---

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Bill → Fixxly order conversion below 45% | Template path + Fixxly-first split UX; measure in 30-day pilot |
| Real MAC ≠ ~200 | Track daily unique orderers; update bridge with actuals |
| Scale paid ads before retention works | Stay **field-first** at 60 OPD; cap paid mix |

**30-day pilot success criteria:** Bill → order **≥45%** · D14 retention (bill users) **≥80%** · Orders/MAC **+30%** vs holdout.
