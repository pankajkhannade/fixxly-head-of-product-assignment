## The one thing I would change about this assignment if I were setting it

Examples: where contractors buy materials after order 1 (dealer vs Fixxly), bill-to-order conversion for Bill Banayein, and daily unique orderers to ground MAC. The rest of the brief — one sharp bet, a working AI prototype, and a unit economics case — I would keep as-is.
